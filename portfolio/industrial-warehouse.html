Engineering & Execution

The facility was built using advanced PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) technology, allowing for clear-span heights and optimized internal volume. The flooring was executed with Laser-Screed technology to ensure FM2 flatness specifications required for automated forklifts and racking systems. We also installed comprehensive fire hazard monitoring and automated fire suppression systems throughout the logistics block.
