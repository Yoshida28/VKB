Design & Execution

The renovation focused on maximizing natural light and internal air circulation through structural wall removals and the addition of floor-to-ceiling glass partitions. Premium Italian marble, custom-built teakwood modular units, and a centrally controlled smart-home lighting system were integrated seamlessly. The project involved a complete plumbing and electrical re-wiring to meet modern safety and load efficiency standards.
