Common Inquiries

Answers to Your Most Common Questions

1. What are your core services?

We offer four core services: Civil Construction (including planning, architectural design, and interior works), Industrial Services, Manpower Supply, and Electrical Services — covering residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

2. Do you supply manpower for industrial and construction sites?

Yes. We provide trained and verified skilled and unskilled workforce — including machine operators, welders, fabricators, electricians, civil workers, and site supervisors — for short-term and long-duration projects.

3. Do you handle electrical work across all project types?

Absolutely. Our electrical team handles installation, maintenance, and repair of wiring, lighting, control panels, earthing, and distribution networks across residential, commercial, and industrial environments — with full compliance and safety checks.
